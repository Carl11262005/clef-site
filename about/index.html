About Us · Our Mission

Good Music Starts
With People.

Our mission is to bring venues, musicians, and bands together—and make the work behind live music simpler.

We’re building one place to discover each other, start conversations, and keep booking details organized. Venues can find artists who fit their room. Musicians and bands can put their music forward and explore new stages.

We want more time and energy to go into the music, the people, and the night you’re creating together.

A Founder’s Note

For The People
Behind Every Show.

I’ve been a jazz pianist my whole life, so music has always been personal to me.

Whether you’re bringing your sound to a room or giving artists a stage, you’re part of what makes live music matter.

I’m building CLEF to make it easier for us to find each other—and spend more time on the music.

Carl PerryCEO and Founder, CLEF
